# Crew catalog

The native picker now offers eight crews.
Batch one adds Heart, Whitebeard, Roger, and Kid with named members beyond each crew's best-known trio.
The lists below follow character allocation order after the captain.
When a roster runs out, numbered generic pirates continue without limiting the number of agents.
Existing assignments remain stable within each crew.

## Heart Pirates

Captain: Trafalgar Law.
Ship: Polar Tang.
Named workers: 8.

Bepo, Shachi, Penguin, Jean Bart, Ikkaku, Uni, Clione, and Hakugan.

The [Heart Pirates reference](https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Heart_Pirates) documents the named roster.
Official profiles also identify [Bepo](https://one-piece.com/character/Bepo/index.html) and [Ikkaku](https://one-piece.com/character/ikkaku/index.html).
The [Polar Tang reference](https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Polar_Tang) documents its yellow submarine design.
The artwork uses its furled-sail configuration.

## Whitebeard Pirates

Captain: Edward Newgate, known as Whitebeard.
Ship: Moby Dick.
Named worker slots: 27.

- Division commanders: Marco, Portgas D. Ace, Jozu, Thatch, Vista, Blamenco, Rakuyo, Namur, Blenheim, Curiel, Kingdew, Haruta, Atmos, Speed Jiru, Fossa, and Izo.
- Other member: Tate.
- Historical members and companions: Kozuki Oden, Kozuki Toki, young Inuarashi, young Nekomamushi, young Marshall D. Teach, young Whitey Bay, young Andre, young Epoida, young Kinga, and Islewan.

The [Whitebeard Pirates reference](https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Whitebeard_Pirates) distinguishes commanders, other members, and historical membership.
The [Moby Dick reference](https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Moby_Dick) documents the whale-shaped flagship.
The historical artwork follows the characters' earlier appearances with Whitebeard.
This roster spans different periods and does not depict a simultaneous crew.
Toki represents a documented traveling companion within this historical selection.

## Roger Pirates

Captain: Gol D. Roger.
Ship: Oro Jackson.
Named worker slots: 31.

- Rayleigh, Scopper Gaban, Crocus, Kozuki Oden, Seagull Guns Nozdon, Sunbell, Spencer, Moon Isaac Jr., Donquino, and Millet Pine.
- Rowing, Erio, Petermoo, Jacksonbanner, Blumarine, MAX Marx, Taro, Doringo, Ganryu, and CB Gallant.
- Mr. Momora, Yui, Rangram, Mugren, Bankuro, Yamon, young Shanks, young Buggy, young Inuarashi, young Nekomamushi, and Kozuki Toki.

The [Roger Pirates reference](https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Roger_Pirates) documents membership and traveling companions.
[SBS Volume 96](https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/SBS_Volume_96), the author's reader-question section, names and sketches the less-prominent members.
The [Oro Jackson reference](https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Oro_Jackson) documents its red sails, mermaid figureheads, forward cannon, and large egg.
Shanks, Buggy, Inuarashi, and Nekomamushi use their young appearances from Roger's era.
Toki represents a traveling companion rather than an appointed crew officer.
Douglas Bullet is excluded because his movie role does not establish manga membership.
Momonosuke and Hiyori are excluded from worker allocation because they traveled as young dependents.

The [Kid Pirates reference](https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Kid_Pirates) lists the expanded roster.

## Artwork interpretation

The sheets preserve identifying silhouettes, clothing, accessories, and known colors within the existing compact character style.
Minor members known mainly through monochrome sketches use interpreted colors.
The artwork is not an official color reference.
Historical membership is an appearance option, not a claim about current story status.
Shared historical characters reuse the same source sheet across crews.
